What's new in 3.2.0

- Download speech models to transcribe completed recordings on your device, without sending audio to the cloud.
- Personalise dictation with corrections learned from edits to text inserted by the Just Speak Keyboard.
- Pair with your Mac and send finished transcripts directly from History.
- Choose your preferred microphone, including a connected headset or USB microphone.
- See more detail in History, including which models were used and how long each step took.
- Find help with permissions, API keys and on-device speech in the new Troubleshooting page.
- Browse more transcription providers and discover OpenRouter models for transcription and text clean-up.
- Recover interrupted recordings more easily, with clearer errors when recording cannot start or a model is unavailable.
